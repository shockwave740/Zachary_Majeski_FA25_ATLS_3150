Definition Lists

A definition list is a list of terms and their definitions. Below is an example of a definition list for some common insects.

Ladybug
A small, round beetle often red with black spots; considered a gardener's friend for its appetite for aphids.
Bee
A flying insect known for its role in pollination and producing honey; has a fuzzy body and can sting when threatened.
Dragonfly
A fast-flying insect with a long body and two pairs of strong, transparent wings; often found near water.

Mantis
Green, angular predator with powerful front legs. Sometimes patient, but strikes lightning fast when hunting. Big fan of staying perfectly still.
Beetle
A very sturdy insect with hard wing covers; travels slowly but steadily to find food and shelter.
Ant
Its strength is bottomless compared to its size.

Conservation

Pollinators like bees and butterflies are essential for biodiversity and food production.

Habitat loss and pesticide use threaten many insect species.
